Your Mission Brief

Welcome to Supernova—an upcoming space-themed YSWS (You Ship, We Ship) program with a flagship hackathon, Interstellar. Whether you're coding software simulations or soldering physical payloads, every hour you ship evolves your project and earns you rewards.

• Space Games and Web Apps

• Satellite Building Kits

• Secret Shop! (Only for Comets)
